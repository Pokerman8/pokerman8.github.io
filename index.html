Ji-Ping Jin

I am an energetic and adventurous M.S. student in Computer Science at ShanghaiTech University, currently focusing on 3D human mesh recovery. I am very fortunate to be advised by Dr. Rui Fan at ShanghaiTech, and I am also a research intern at IntelInDust, where I work with Dr. Xi Shen on advanced on cutting-edge projects in human-centric vision.

At the same time, I maintain an open and positive attitude toward AI for medical applications, and I am eager to adapt human-centric vision techniques to clinical and health-related scenarios.
